When it comes to exfoliation, you have two main options: chemical or physical. Both have their place in a skincare routine, but I created the Wizker brush because I believe physical exfoliation — done right — is the most effective and accessible approach for most people.

## Chemical vs. Physical Exfoliation

Chemical exfoliants (AHAs, BHAs, enzymes) dissolve the bonds between dead skin cells, allowing them to shed naturally. They're effective but can be irritating, especially for sensitive skin, and they require careful layering with other products.

Physical exfoliants work differently — they manually lift and remove dead skin cells through friction. The key is control. Harsh scrubs with abrasive particles can cause micro-tears. But a well-designed brush delivers controlled, consistent exfoliation without that risk.

## The Bigger Picture

Regular exfoliation does more than remove dead skin. It allows your other skincare products — serums, moisturizers, treatments — to absorb properly instead of sitting on top of a layer of buildup. It's the foundation that makes everything else in your routine work better.
